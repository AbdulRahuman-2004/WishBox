# 💜 WishBox – Beautiful Messages for Every Occasion

Welcome to **WishBox**, a simple and creative website where you can create and share personalized messages for the people who matter most.

Whether it's love, friendship, birthdays, anniversaries, thank-you messages or just a special moment, WishBox helps you express your feelings through beautiful words and greeting images.

## ✨ Features

- 💕 Personalized love messages
- 🎂 Birthday wishes
- 👫 Friendship messages
- 🌸 Thank-you messages
- 💌 Special occasion greetings
- ✍️ Create customized messages
- 🎨 Beautiful greeting image generation
- 📤 Share messages and greeting images
- 🔗 Share your website link with messages
- 💜 Attractive and user-friendly design

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the WishBox website.
2. Choose your preferred occasion.
3. Enter the required details.
4. Generate your personalized message.
5. View your greeting message and image.
6. Share it with your friends, family or loved ones.
